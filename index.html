THE LAST FRAME

A campus OSINT murder mystery

This is a work of fiction. All characters, accounts and events are imaginary and not based on any real student, staff member or incident.

The case

The fest is over, and the campus is quiet.

At 6:15 this morning, the security guard found Rohan Menon on the ground below the campus terrace. Rohan was a final-year student and the fest’s media head, and the person with a camera at every event. The terrace had been closed off for repairs, and his phone is missing. Police say he died between 11:00 PM and midnight.

In his last two weeks, Rohan’s posts turned strange. There were cropped screenshots, cryptic captions and a message nobody understood. He had found something, and he told someone.

Three people were close to Rohan and the fest:

  • Kabir Nair @kabir.codes
    roommate and best friend
  • Manish Iyer @manish.speaks
    cultural committee head
  • Dev Malhotra @dev.after.dark
    fest DJ and Rohan’s former best friend

Each of them has an alibi on their page, and only one of them is lying.

Your mission

Study the four Instagram pages (posts, stories and highlights), answer the 10 questions, and name the killer. Then prove it with one piece of evidence that can’t be explained away.

The rules

  1. Ignore Instagram’s real upload times. Only what is inside the pictures counts, such as names, handles, numbers, backgrounds and clocks shown on a screen.
  2. Look closely. Check captions, comments, highlights, bios, backgrounds and the last slide of every carousel.
  3. Use only these four accounts. Everything you need is on them, so don’t go looking elsewhere or contact anyone.
  4. Not every suspicious clue points to the killer. Some people look guilty for reasons that have nothing to do with the murder.
